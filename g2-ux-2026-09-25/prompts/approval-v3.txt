Flat UI concept frame: the raw display buffer of a monochrome heads-up display, shown straight-on and full-bleed, 2:1 aspect. This is a design mock of what the pixels show, not a photo of glasses.

DISPLAY PHYSICS: a single-color green microLED panel. Pure black background means transparent (see-through), so the frame is black except lit green pixels. ONE green hue only (#3CFF7A family) at three brightness levels: bright, medium, dim. No other colors, no gradients, no glow halos, no shadows. Crisp pixel-font monospace text on a strict grid, margins about 6% on every side.

LAYOUT: an approval card, EXACTLY ONE 1-pixel medium-green rounded rectangle outline filling most of the frame.
Inside the card, line 1, dim, small: "studio · navier · Bash"
Line 2, bright and LARGEST (about 14% of frame height), the hero, left-aligned: "git push origin main"
Line 3, medium: "push 3 commits to origin"
Bottom row of the card, EXACTLY TWO choices: left "◂ deny" in bright, drawn inverted (black text on a solid green pill) because it holds the default focus; right "allow ▸" in medium, outline only.
Below the card, dim, centered: "▲▼ choose   ● confirm"

Text sizes: every line at least 7% of frame height. No extra words, no duplicate text, no other labels or icons.
STYLE: restrained, still, Japanese-minimal instrument display; the command string is the hero, nothing competes with it.
