Flat UI concept frame: the raw display buffer of a monochrome heads-up display, shown straight-on and full-bleed, 2:1 aspect. This is a design mock of what the pixels show, not a photo of glasses.

DISPLAY PHYSICS: a single-color green microLED panel. Pure black background means transparent (see-through), so the frame is black everywhere except lit green pixels. Use ONE green hue only (#3CFF7A family) at three brightness levels: bright for the focused row, medium for normal text, dim for hints. No other colors anywhere. No gradients, no glow halos, no shadows, no glass effects. Crisp pixel-font monospace text, aligned to a strict grid, generous margins (about 6% on every side).

Header row, left: a tiny 1-bit pixel-art penguin icon (EXACTLY ONE, two round eyes, small snow pile on its head, about 5% of frame height) followed by the text "studio · claude". Header right: "3 sessions".
A 1-pixel dim horizontal hairline under the header.
EXACTLY THREE session rows in a left-aligned monospace table with three columns (name, state, detail):

Text sizes: every text line is at least 7% of frame height. No extra words, no duplicate text, no other labels, no icons except the one penguin and the caret.
STYLE: restrained, still, Japanese-minimal instrument display; reads like a precise avionics glance.
