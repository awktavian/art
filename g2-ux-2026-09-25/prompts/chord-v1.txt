Flat UI concept frame: the raw display buffer of a monochrome heads-up display, shown straight-on and full-bleed, 2:1 aspect. Design mock of pixels, not a photo.

DISPLAY PHYSICS: single-color green microLED. Pure black = transparent, so the frame is black except lit green pixels. ONE green hue only (#3CFF7A family) at three brightness levels. No other colors, no gradients, no glow, no shadows. Crisp pixel-font monospace on a strict grid, margins about 6%.

LAYOUT: a chorded text-entry overlay.
Top-left, dim: "chord entry · concept"
Center-top, bright and large (about 12% of frame height), an input line with a block cursor: "git sta█"
Below it, a row of EXACTLY FOUR suggestion chips, each a 1-pixel medium outline rectangle with medium text: "status" "stash list" "stage" "start". The first chip "status" is bright and filled (inverted: black text on green).
Bottom area: a compact chord legend as EXACTLY FIVE columns, each column a vertical stack of three small dots (filled ● or hollow ○) above a single label. Labels left to right: "a" "e" "t" "space" "enter". Dots dim green.
Bottom-right, dim: "hold = shift"

Every text line at least 6% of frame height. No extra words, no duplicate text, no other icons.
STYLE: restrained, still, avionics-precise, Japanese-minimal.
